# Teaching staff: phân công giảng dạy, kiêm nhiệm và định mức, lịch báo giảng, sổ chủ nhiệm

Phase 10 adds what SMAS keeps about teachers' work and this system did not: who teaches each
subject in each class (phân công chuyên môn), who is homeroom teacher of each class, the positions
and concurrent duties teachers hold with the teaching norm of Thông tư 05/2025/TT-BGDĐT, the
teacher's week of lessons (lịch báo giảng), and the homeroom teacher's book (sổ chủ nhiệm),
written in the portal and printed.

## Phân công giảng dạy

*Cán bộ, giáo viên › Phân công giảng dạy* is a grid of classes and subjects for one semester. Each
cell holds the teachers of the subject in the class and their periods a week. The school's leaders
(admin) set it; every portal user reads it.

- A cell takes up to four teachers, each with periods a week in half periods, so a subject can be
  shared (the parts of Khoa học tự nhiên). A teacher who no longer works at the school is refused.
- *Lấy từ thời khóa biểu* makes the assignments of every subject of a class that has none yet,
  with the teachers and the number of periods on the timetable. *Sao chép từ học kỳ I* fills the
  second semester the same way from the first.
- A cell with no assignment shows the timetable's teachers in grey. A warning marks a cell where
  the timetable has another teacher or a different number of periods. The cell editor suggests the
  periods a week from the subject's periods a year (35 weeks, to the nearest half period).
- *Theo giáo viên* lists each teacher with their tổ chuyên môn, subjects, classes and total periods.
- *In bảng phân công* prints the grid (the `teaching-by-class` report).

The class view of *Thời khóa biểu* gains *Đối chiếu phân công giảng dạy*: each subject's assigned
teachers and periods against those on the timetable (*Khớp*, *Thiếu*, *Thừa*, *Khác giáo viên*).
When a period is added there, the assigned teacher is picked first.

**In the gradebook.** Once a semester has any assignment, a teacher writes the marks of a subject
in a class only if they are assigned to it; other teachers see the sheet read-only with the names
of those assigned. Admin and staff still write every sheet. Until the school records assignments
for the semester, the timetable stands in as before. *Giám sát nhập điểm*, the missing-marks list
and the sổ chủ nhiệm also read the assignments, or the timetable before there are any. A
teacher's *Nhập điểm* opens on their first assigned class and subject, with a list to jump between
them.

**Tổ chuyên môn.** The teacher form has a tổ chuyên môn, typed or picked from those the school
already uses, and the teacher list filters by it. The staffing report groups teachers by tổ.

## Phân công chủ nhiệm

*Cán bộ, giáo viên › Phân công chủ nhiệm* lists every class of the year with its homeroom teacher,
saved together, and each teacher's other homeroom classes and duties beside the class. A teacher
who would hold more than two concurrent duties, homeroom classes included, is flagged (Điều 3).

## Chức vụ, kiêm nhiệm và định mức tiết dạy

*Cán bộ, giáo viên › Kiêm nhiệm & định mức* has three tabs.

**Danh mục và định mức.** A school starts from the circular's list for its level, worked out from
the grades of its classes (1 to 5 Tiểu học, 6 to 9 THCS, 10 to 12 THPT):

| Code | Position or duty | Kind | Periods a week | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HT | Hiệu trưởng | position, its own norm | 2 | Điều 8 |
| PHT | Phó hiệu trưởng | position, its own norm | 4 | Điều 8 |
| TPTD | Tổng phụ trách Đội (TH and THCS) | position, its own norm | TH 8, THCS 6 | Điều 7 |
| TTCM | Tổ trưởng chuyên môn | concurrent, reduction | 3 | Điều 9 |
| TPCM | Tổ phó chuyên môn | concurrent, reduction | 1 | Điều 9 |
| PHBM | Phụ trách phòng học bộ môn | concurrent, reduction | 3 | Điều 9 |
| PTB | Phụ trách phòng thiết bị giáo dục | concurrent, reduction | 3 | Điều 9 |
| BTCB | Bí thư chi bộ | concurrent, reduction | 3 | Điều 10 |
| CTHD | Chủ tịch hội đồng trường | concurrent, reduction | 2 | Điều 10 |
| TKHD | Thư ký hội đồng trường | concurrent, reduction | 2 | Điều 10 |
| TBTT | Trưởng ban thanh tra nhân dân | concurrent, reduction | 2 | Điều 10 |
| CNTT | Kiêm nhiệm công nghệ thông tin | concurrent, reduction | 3 | Điều 11 |
| VT | Kiêm nhiệm văn thư | concurrent, reduction | 3 | Điều 11 |
| TV | Kiêm nhiệm thư viện | concurrent, reduction | 3 | Điều 11 |
| TS | Giáo viên tập sự | other reduction | 2 | Điều 12 |
| NCN | Giáo viên nữ nuôi con dưới 12 tháng | other reduction | TH 4, others 3 | Điều 12 |

The school changes the periods, adds its own items and stops using those it no longer needs. An
item that teachers hold keeps its kind and cannot be deleted, only stopped. The teacher's norm is
23 periods a week in a primary school, 19 in a THCS and 17 in a THPT (Điều 7), and a homeroom
teacher is relieved of 4 (Điều 9). A school with norms of its own (a boarding school, a school for
students with disabilities) sets them and can go back to the circular's.

**Chức vụ, kiêm nhiệm.** Each teacher's positions and duties for the whole year or one semester,
with the catalogue's periods or a number of their own (a Tổng phụ trách Đội at a large school)
and a note (which tổ, which room). The same duty cannot be held twice in overlapping periods.

**Định mức tiết dạy.** For each teacher in a semester: the norm (a position's own norm, else the
teacher's), the reductions (homeroom classes, concurrent and other duties), the periods they must
teach, the periods assigned and the difference, over (+) or short (-). Three warnings are raised:
more than two concurrent duties with homeroom classes counted, more than half the norm taught over
it (both Điều 3), and two positions with norms of their own, where the lower norm applies.

## Lịch báo giảng

*Cán bộ, giáo viên › Lịch báo giảng* shows a teacher's week from the timetable, Monday to Saturday,
with the school week number. Week 1 is the week the school year opens in, or the next one when it
opens on a Saturday or Sunday. For each period the teacher writes the lesson number of the
subject's plan (tiết PPCT), the lesson title, the teaching aids and a note, and saves the week.

- Make-up and substitute periods outside the timetable are added by hand, and an empty title takes
  a period off the calendar.
- Teachers write their own week; the school's leaders (admin) can write anyone's; staff and other
  teachers read it. The week prints as the `lesson-calendar` report, signed by the teacher.
- A teacher's dashboard shows this week's periods, green where the lesson is written, with a button
  to the missing ones, and their homeroom classes with links to the roll call and the sổ chủ nhiệm.
- In the sổ đầu bài, a new entry starts from the lesson written for the period, as
  "Tiết 18: Bài 9. Dấu hiệu chia hết".

## Sổ chủ nhiệm

*Lớp học › Sổ chủ nhiệm* is the homeroom teacher's book of the class for the school year. Teachers
see the classes they are homeroom teacher of; admin and staff see every class. The homeroom
teacher writes it and the school's leaders (admin) may too; staff only read it. It has eight tabs:

- **Thông tin chung**: the subject teachers of each semester (from the assignments, or the
  timetable), the students with date of birth, ethnicity, diện chính sách, parents and phone, and
  the class's situation counted from the records: sĩ số, girls, ethnic minorities, religion, Đội or
  Đoàn members, diện chính sách, and how the students finished the previous year.
- **Cán sự lớp, ban đại diện**: the class officers, students of the class in any role, and the
  parents' committee, parents of the class's students with one role each.
- **Tổ và chỗ ngồi**: the tổ with their leader, each student in one tổ, split automatically or by
  hand, and the seating chart of dãy, bàn and chỗ, filled from the list or by tổ and then by hand.
- **Kế hoạch chủ nhiệm**: the year plan (đặc điểm tình hình, mục tiêu giáo dục, chỉ tiêu phấn đấu,
  biện pháp thực hiện) and a plan for each month with its theme, tasks and review.
- **Theo dõi học sinh**: students the teacher follows, as needing attention, outstanding or making
  progress, with the measure taken and the result.
- **Họp cha mẹ học sinh**: the minutes of each meeting with the parents present out of those
  invited, their opinions and the conclusions.
- **Kết quả, chuyên cần**: each student's academic and conduct results for each semester and the
  year, title, days off with and without leave, commendations and discipline, all read from the
  gradebook, the roll call and the records.
- **Ý kiến của Ban giám hiệu**: the principal's or a vice principal's dated review. Only its writer
  deletes it.

A student who leaves the class drops out of the officers, tổ and seats. Dates must fall in the
school year or the summer after it, up to 31 August. *In sổ* prints the book in twelve numbered
sections, signed by the homeroom teacher.

## Báo cáo

| Report | Group | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `teaching-assignments` Bảng phân công chuyên môn: each teacher's positions, duties, subjects, classes and periods against the norm, by tổ | Cán bộ, giáo viên | portal |
| `teaching-by-class` Bảng phân công giảng dạy theo lớp, for a semester and optionally a grade | Cán bộ, giáo viên | portal |
| `lesson-calendar` Lịch báo giảng of a teacher's week | Cán bộ, giáo viên | portal |
| `homeroom-book` Sổ chủ nhiệm of a class | Công tác chủ nhiệm | homeroom teacher, admin, staff |

## Routes

| Route | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /teaching/assignments?semester=&classId=&teacherId=` | portal | assignments, the timetable's periods and suggested periods |
| `PUT /teaching/assignments` | admin | `{ classId, subjectId, semester, teachers: [{ teacherId, periodsPerWeek }] }`; an empty list clears the cell |
| `POST /teaching/assignments/from-timetable`, `/copy` | admin | `{ semester }`, `{ from, to }` |
| `GET /teaching/homeroom`, `PUT` | portal; admin | homeroom teachers of the year; `{ items: [{ classId, teacherId }] }` |
| `GET /teaching/duty-types`, `POST`, `PATCH /:id`, `DELETE /:id` | portal; admin | the catalogue |
| `GET /teaching/duties?teacherId=`, `POST`, `PATCH /:id`, `DELETE /:id` | portal; admin | positions and duties held |
| `GET /teaching/workload?semester=` | portal | each teacher's norm, reductions, load and warnings |
| `GET /teaching/workload/settings`, `PUT`, `DELETE` | portal; admin | the school's norms; back to the circular's |
| `GET /teaching/calendar?teacherId=&date=` | portal | a teacher's week |
| `PUT /teaching/calendar` | the teacher, admin | `{ teacherId?, entries: [{ date, periodNumber, classId, subjectId, lessonNo?, title, aids?, note? }] }` |
| `GET /homeroom/book?classId=`, `PUT` | homeroom teacher, admin, staff (read) | the book; officers, committee, tổ, seating, year plan |
| `PUT /homeroom/book/month-plans`, `DELETE /:id` | homeroom teacher, admin | a month's plan |
| `POST /homeroom/book/meetings`, `PATCH /:id`, `DELETE /:id` | homeroom teacher, admin | parent meetings |
| `POST /homeroom/book/notes`, `PATCH /:id`, `DELETE /:id` | homeroom teacher, admin | students followed |
| `POST /homeroom/book/reviews`, `DELETE /:id` | admin | the leaders' review |

## Demo data

The seed sorts the demo teachers into two tổ chuyên môn (Toán - Khoa học tự nhiên and Ngữ văn -
Khoa học xã hội) and adds the principal (`hieutruong@demo.edu.vn`) and a vice principal as teachers
who also teach. It gives the year's duties: tổ trưởng and tổ phó, thư ký hội đồng, trưởng ban thanh
tra, phụ trách phòng bộ môn, công nghệ thông tin with the computer room, thiết bị, Tổng phụ trách
Đội and thư viện. One teacher holds three concurrent duties and is flagged. Both semesters'
assignments come from the timetable, and from the programme's periods for the subjects it does not
hold. GV001 has lịch báo giảng for last week and this week, with the last period of this week still
to write. 6A1's sổ chủ nhiệm has its officers, parents' committee, three tổ and a seating chart,
the year plan, September's and October's plans, the first parent meeting, five students followed
and the principal's review. A demo database seeded before this phase gets the same data on its next
start, as long as it has no teaching assignments and no sổ chủ nhiệm yet, because the top-up never
deletes anything.
